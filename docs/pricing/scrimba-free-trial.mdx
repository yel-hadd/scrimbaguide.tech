## Does Scrimba have a free trial?

Not in the usual SaaS sense. There is no countdown that hands you full Pro for a week and locks you out after. People search for a "Scrimba free trial" because they want to test the product before paying, and free gets you there: the free account is permanent, not a teaser, so you can learn on Scrimba for months without spending anything.

### 1. The free account

This is the closest thing to an unlimited free trial, because it does not expire. Create a free account and you get {freeCountLabel} full courses, up to 10 interactive coding challenges, 10 [Scrimba Explain](/docs/how-it-works/scrimba-explain/) explainers, weekly live events, and the community. No credit card, no clock. You can finish entire courses on the free tier and never pay: [Learn JavaScript](/docs/courses/javascript/learn-javascript/) (9.4 hours of video, 238 lessons, from a passenger counter you deploy to Netlify through blackjack and a Chrome extension) and [Learn React](/docs/courses/react/learn-react/) (15.1 hours, 358 lessons, both capstone games included) are free from first scrim to last.

The limits are clear ones: career paths, unlimited challenges, the Pro-only Discord channels (basic Discord access is free), and path certificates sit behind Pro. Inside the free courses, the only Pro-marked items are the solo projects: Learn JavaScript's basketball scoreboard, password generator and unit converter, and Learn React's Digital Business Card and Quizzical. For the full breakdown of where free ends, read [Is Scrimba free? Pro vs Free explained](/docs/pricing/pro-vs-free/).

Create a <AffiliateLink href="https://scrimba.com/?via=u42d4986" location="free-trial-account">free account</AffiliateLink> and you can start on either course today, no card required.

### 2. The no-signup demo lesson

If even making an account feels like commitment, skip it. Scrimba runs a public demo scrim that opens a real, editable lesson in your browser with no login. Pause the video, change the instructor's code, hit run, and you have felt the one thing that makes Scrimba different from passive video. It takes under a minute and it is the fastest test of whether the format clicks for you: open the <AffiliateLink href="https://scrimba.com/s0v687325e" location="free-trial-demo">demo lesson</AffiliateLink> and try it yourself.

### 3. The 7-day guarantee

This is the route people actually mean when they search for a free trial of Pro. Scrimba backs a first Pro purchase with a 7-day money-back guarantee, no questions asked. Subscribe, unlock every course, path, and challenge, work through the parts you care about for a week, and request a full refund if it does not fit how you learn. Be realistic about what a week covers: the Pro-only [Learn Responsive Web Design](/docs/courses/css/responsive-web-design/) is 15.1 hours of video and the review on this site budgets 30 to 45 hours for it. Use the week to sample the Pro courses and solo projects, not to finish them.

Two caveats: you pay up front and wait for the refund, and the guarantee is meant for genuine first-time evaluation, not serial refunding. The mechanics, including how to request it, are in the [refund policy](/docs/pricing/refund-policy/). Open <AffiliateLink href="https://scrimba.com/?via=u42d4986" location="free-trial-pro">Scrimba Pro</AffiliateLink> to start the clock.

### 4. Students: one free month of Pro via GitHub

If you are a verified student, this is the closest thing to a true free trial of Pro. Scrimba is part of the [GitHub Student Developer Pack](https://education.github.com/pack), which includes one free month of Scrimba Pro.

1. Apply for the [GitHub Student Developer Pack](https://education.github.com/pack). You usually need a school email or a photo of your enrollment ID. GitHub does the verifying.
2. Once approved, open the <AffiliateLink location="inline-scrimba-free-trial-2" href="https://scrimba.com/github-education?via=u42d4986">Scrimba GitHub Education page</AffiliateLink> and sign in with GitHub.
3. In the checkout modal, find the GitHub Student 30-day offer and click upgrade to activate your free Pro month.

The free month is one-time, not recurring, so you fall back to free when it ends unless you subscribe. Full detail and the academic-email discount are on the [student discount](/docs/pricing/student-discount/) page.

## What about coupon codes?

There is no universal public coupon for Scrimba. If a coupon site shows you "SCRIMBA50," treat it as fiction unless it lives on Scrimba's own domain. The two durable savings are the student paths above and the standing partner discount our links apply at checkout. We track any live, time-boxed promotions in the [2026 discount codes guide](/blog/scrimba-discount-codes-2026/); seasonal sales cluster around Black Friday and New Year, not random codes.
