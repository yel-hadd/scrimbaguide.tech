# Scrimba Pricing

<DisclosureNotice />

Scrimba has a free tier with about {freeCount} full courses, no credit card required, and a Pro subscription that unlocks the four career paths, unlimited coding challenges, Pro-only Discord channels, and path certificates. ## Should you pay for Scrimba Pro?

Pro buys a structured route from "I cannot read an error" to "I shipped a portfolio app": the four career paths, unlimited challenges, and the Pro Discord channels. Free is enough to test whether the format works for you first.

> **Low risk:** 7-day refund on first purchase, cancel anytime, progress saved if you pause.

Scrimba supports purchasing power parity (PPP), so a price in a lower-income country should already come in lower automatically. The third-party site [paritylist.com/products/scrimba](https://www.paritylist.com/products/scrimba) tracks how much the platform discounts by country.

## What the free tier gets you

The free tier is not a trial. It is a permanent product with real courses. If you came looking for a [Scrimba free trial](/docs/pricing/scrimba-free-trial/), that page lays out the four free ways in, including the student free month. The most-taken free courses are:

- **[Learn React](/docs/courses/react/learn-react/)** with Bob Ziroll. 15.1 hours of video, 358 lessons, built with Mozilla MDN's input. All six sections and both capstone games (Tenzies and Assembly: Endgame) are free. This is the course most people land on Scrimba for, and it is the strongest argument that the free tier is worth opening even if you never pay.
- **[Learn JavaScript](/docs/courses/javascript/learn-javascript/)** with Per Borgen. 9.4 hours, 238 lessons. The passenger counter, blackjack, Chrome extension and Firebase modules are all free, deploys included.
- **[Learn TypeScript](/docs/courses/typescript/learn-typescript/)** with Bob Ziroll and Rachel Johnson. 4.2 hours, 79 lessons, free.
- **[Learn HTML and CSS](/docs/courses/css/html-and-css/)** with Per Borgen. 5.7 hours, 121 lessons. Free, and so is its final solo project, the Hometown Homepage.
- **[Learn Python](/docs/courses/python/learn-python/)**. 4.1 hours, 133 lessons, with no Pro items inside it at all.
- **[Intro to Mistral AI](/docs/courses/ai/mistral-ai/)** (1.4 hours) and **Build Serverless AI Agents (Langbase)** (50 minutes). Quick AI intros, free.

You also get ten interactive coding challenges total. That is the cap, ten across the whole platform, not ten per course. Once you hit it, you are nudged toward Pro. [Scrimba Explain](/docs/how-it-works/scrimba-explain/), the AI explainer button inside every scrim, gives a free account 10 explainers for the life of the account.

Inside the free courses, the one thing marked Pro is the solo project. In [Learn JavaScript](/docs/courses/javascript/learn-javascript/) that means the basketball scoreboard, the password generator and the unit converter, each with a Figma file, a written spec and stretch goals. In [Learn React](/docs/courses/react/learn-react/) it is the Digital Business Card and Quizzical. Every lesson around them plays without a card.

You do **not** get the four career paths, the Pro-only Discord channels (basic Discord access is free), unlimited challenges, or certificates of completion for paths.

## What Pro unlocks, and what you use it for

Pro is one subscription that includes everything paid on the platform. The four career paths are the centre of the value, and they are why most people upgrade:

- **[Frontend Developer Path](/docs/paths/frontend-developer-path/)**, built with Mozilla MDN. Roughly 82 hours of lessons across 13 modules. HTML, CSS, JavaScript, accessibility, React, working with APIs, a portfolio-building module, and a getting-hired module. This is the most-enrolled path and the one that frames most "did Scrimba help me get hired" discussions.
- **[Fullstack Developer Path](/docs/paths/fullstack-developer-path/)**. Roughly 108 hours. Folds backend, databases, TypeScript, and a chunk of AI engineering onto the frontend foundation.
- **[Backend Developer Path](/docs/paths/backend-developer-path/)**. Roughly 36 hours. Node, Express, SQL, Git, cybersecurity, DevOps. Assumes you already know frontend.
- **[AI Engineer Path](/docs/paths/ai-engineer-path/)**. Roughly 11 hours so far. Agents, RAG, MCP, Vercel AI SDK, Cloudflare deployment. The smallest path and the most actively expanding.

Some standalone courses are Pro as well, with only a few sample scrims open: [Advanced React](/docs/courses/react/advanced-react/) (18.8 hours, three samples), React Router, [Learn Responsive Web Design](/docs/courses/css/responsive-web-design/) (15.1 hours, four samples), [Learn CSS Grid](/docs/courses/css/css-grid/) and [Learn Flexbox](/docs/courses/css/flexbox/) (three and four samples), [Intro to AI Engineering](/docs/courses/ai/ai-engineering/) and [Prompt Engineering for Web Developers](/docs/courses/ai/prompt-engineering-for-web-developers/) (five samples each), and [JavaScript Interview Challenges](/docs/courses/javascript/javascript-interview-challenges/) (two samples).

You also get unlimited coding challenges (not the ten-total cap), the Pro-only Discord channels (code reviews, instructor and career channels) on top of the basic Discord access every free account has, certificates of completion for paths you finish (LinkedIn-shareable), and any Pro courses Scrimba adds during your subscription period. Pro also raises the [Scrimba Explain](/docs/how-it-works/scrimba-explain/) quota from 10 AI video explainers for life to 100 a month, and the monthly allowance renews.

## Annual vs monthly

Annual billing has the lower per-month rate, roughly half of the monthly rate. Check the exact ratio on the official page since it moves with promotions. Study consistently for three months or more and annual pays for itself.

If you want flexibility (a sabbatical month, an exam period), the monthly plan has no commitment beyond the current month and can be cancelled from the account settings. You keep access until the end of the paid period and your progress is preserved if you come back.

## Refunds

Scrimba publishes a seven-day money-back guarantee on first purchase. See the [official refund article](https://scrimba.helpscoutdocs.com/article/6-how-do-i-get-a-refund) for the canonical policy, or our [refund policy summary](/docs/pricing/refund-policy/) for the readable version.

## Where you can save

Four legitimate ways to pay less:

1. **Annual billing.** The simplest and largest discount versus paying monthly.
2. **Student discount.** Available with a valid academic email. The discount percentage varies by promotion; the [student discount guide](/docs/pricing/student-discount/) walks through the eligibility check.
3. **GitHub Student Developer Pack.** One free month of Pro for verified students. Worth claiming if you are eligible.
4. **Partner discount.** The affiliate link on this page applies a discount at checkout (typically 20% off Pro). A one-time banner confirms the discount when you land on Scrimba; you can close it and keep exploring courses for free, no purchase required. It works for new subscribers; verify the final price before paying.

There is **no universal public coupon** for Scrimba. Coupon-aggregator sites that promise "Scrimba 50% off codes" are recycling expired promotions or affiliate partner links. If a number looks too good, it is.

## How Scrimba compares to a coding bootcamp

Bootcamp tuition runs from about $3,500 to $30,000 for a few months of full-time instruction plus career services, and the average sits around $14,000, per [Course Report's bootcamp guide](https://www.coursereport.com/coding-bootcamp-ultimate-guide). That is the comparison most "is Scrimba worth it" discussions have in mind.

Scrimba is two orders of magnitude cheaper, and it has none of the things bootcamps charge for: real-time instruction, a cohort to study with, a designated career coach, employer relationships. Self-motivated learners with momentum can substitute Scrimba for a bootcamp. Learners who need accountability, or who would not finish a self-paced course without external pressure, get more out of a bootcamp's structure.

A useful side-by-side is on the [Scrimba vs Bootcamps](/docs/pricing/scrimba-vs-bootcamps/) page.

## Where Pro isn't worth it

- **JavaScript-centric.** Scrimba is built around the JavaScript and TypeScript stack, with Python as a second-tier option. If your career goal is Java, Go, Rust, mobile (Swift/Kotlin), or data science, skip Pro.
- **No live cohorts.** Self-paced video plus Discord, not a scheduled bootcamp.
- **Module-by-module breakdowns are not available for every course.** Where this site has a full module list it shows one; where it does not, the page says so. Always confirm the current curriculum on Scrimba's own course page.
- **Course counts drift.** Old courses get retired, new ones launch. The catalog tracker on this site is updated nightly from Scrimba's structured data and may reflect a different count than Scrimba's own marketing pages on any given day.
