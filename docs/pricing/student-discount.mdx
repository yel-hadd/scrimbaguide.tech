# Scrimba Student Discount

<DisclosureNotice />

**Quick answer:** Three honest paths exist. First, email `help@scrimba.com` from a verified school address and ask for the student rate. Second, claim the [GitHub Student Developer Pack](https://education.github.com/pack) and redeem one free month of Pro on Scrimba's GitHub Education page. Third, check the price you actually see at checkout: Scrimba applies regional pricing automatically based on your location, with no code required. There is no universal public coupon code; Scrimba grants the student discount only after verifying you are actually a student. For current pricing, see the <AffiliateLink href="https://scrimba.com/our-pricing">official Scrimba pricing page</AffiliateLink>.

*Last reviewed: September 2026. Discount terms can change. Confirm with the Scrimba help centre before you act.*

## The three options at a glance

| | GitHub Student Pack | Academic-email discount | Regional pricing |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **What you get** | One free month of Pro | Ongoing reduced Pro rate | A lower price if your country qualifies |
| **Speed** | Fastest (instant once GitHub-approved) | 1 to 3 business days | Instant, applied at checkout |
| **Duration** | 30 days, one-time | While you stay verified | Every payment while you qualify |
| **Eligibility** | Verified GitHub Education students | Anyone with a valid academic email | Based on your location at checkout |
| **How to claim** | Scrimba GitHub Education page | Email `help@scrimba.com` | Nothing to do; it is automatic |

Read the rest of this page for the step-by-step on each, then pick the fastest path that applies to you.

## Who this page is for

Students with a `.edu` (or equivalent national academic) email, anyone already approved for GitHub Education, and anyone checking whether the price they see is already reduced for their region. If you are a self-taught learner without a school enrollment, the two student paths below do not apply, but regional pricing still might; see also the [refund policy](/docs/pricing/refund-policy/) and [pricing page](/docs/pricing/).

## Option 1: academic email discount

If you have a valid academic email address, you can ask Scrimba for a student rate.

1. From your school inbox, email `help@scrimba.com`
2. State that you are a student and request the academic discount
3. Scrimba's team verifies the address and applies the discount manually

This is a manual process. There is no automatic checkout coupon. The amount of the discount and how long it lasts is set by Scrimba and can change, so do not assume a number you read on a blog from last year. Ask support directly.

Reference: [Scrimba Help Centre: Do you offer student discounts?](https://scrimba.helpscoutdocs.com/article/19-do-you-offer-student-discounts)

## Option 2: GitHub Student Developer Pack

The [GitHub Student Developer Pack](https://education.github.com/pack) is a free bundle of tools for verified students. It includes one free month of Scrimba Pro. This is often the simplest path if you already have a developer-leaning student profile.

1. Apply for the [GitHub Student Developer Pack](https://education.github.com/pack). You typically need a school email, a photo of your enrollment ID, or both. GitHub does the verification.
2. Once approved, visit the <AffiliateLink href="https://scrimba.com/github-education?via=u42d4986">Scrimba GitHub Education page</AffiliateLink> and sign in with GitHub.
3. In the checkout modal look for the **GitHub Student 30-Day Trial** message and the yellow **UPGRADE** button. Click it to activate your free Pro month.

Reference: [Scrimba Help Centre: How do I get the Student GitHub free access?](https://scrimba.helpscoutdocs.com/article/46-how-do-i-access-the-student-github-free-access)

The free month is one shot, not recurring. After it ends you fall back to free unless you subscribe. If you also qualify for the academic-email discount, stack them in sequence: use the GitHub free month first, then subscribe at the student rate.

## Option 3: automatic regional pricing

Scrimba adjusts its price by country. You do not need a code, a form, or an email: if your country qualifies, the lower price is simply the price you see at checkout. Prices are displayed in US dollars either way.

One practical catch. A VPN or proxy makes you look like you are connecting from somewhere else, so the price you see will match that location instead of yours. Turn it off before you check. If you believe your country should qualify and you still see the standard price, `help@scrimba.com` is the place to ask.

Reference: [Scrimba Help Centre: Do you offer PPP or regional pricing?](https://scrimba.helpscoutdocs.com/article/110-ppp-regional-pricing)

## Does Scrimba have a scholarship program?

No. Scrimba's help centre states plainly that it does not offer scholarships at this time, and it points students to the GitHub Student Developer Pack instead. Be skeptical of any third-party site advertising a Scrimba scholarship programme or application form.

That does not leave you without options. The GitHub Pack month, the academic-email rate, and regional pricing above are the three routes Scrimba actually documents.

Reference: [Scrimba Help Centre: Can I get a scholarship?](https://scrimba.helpscoutdocs.com/article/71-can-i-get-a-scholarship)

## If you need to stop paying for a while

You can pause a Pro subscription rather than cancel it. Pausing stops future payments while keeping your account and your course progress, so you can restart when money frees up. To resume, or to cancel while paused, email `help@scrimba.com`.

This is worth knowing before you cancel outright, because cancelling and resubscribing later means starting the payment relationship over.

Reference: [Scrimba Help Centre: How do I pause my subscription?](https://scrimba.helpscoutdocs.com/article/103-how-do-i-pause-my-subscription-resume-it-or-cancel-while-paused)

## Which option to pick

- **Use the GitHub Student Pack** if you want to try Pro free for a month before deciding. This is the fastest path and you keep the rest of the GitHub Pack benefits regardless.
- **Use the academic-email discount** if you want an ongoing reduced rate while you study.
- **Check regional pricing** before you assume the listed price applies to you. It costs nothing to look and it needs no approval.
- **Pause instead of cancelling** if you already subscribe and need a break from payments.

## How to actually claim this, this week

Pick the fastest path that applies to you and do it now. If you already have GitHub Student approval, log in to the <AffiliateLink href="https://scrimba.com/github-education">Scrimba GitHub Education page</AffiliateLink> and click the upgrade button; the free Pro month starts immediately. If you only have a school email, draft a short message to `help@scrimba.com` from that inbox today, then plan for a 1 to 3 business day reply before paying anything. If neither applies, start on the free tier while you decide; the <AffiliateLink href="https://scrimba.com/s0v687325e">demo scrim</AffiliateLink> takes under a minute and there is no signup. Learn JavaScript (238 lessons) and Learn React (358 lessons) are free in full, so a month of waiting on verification is not a month lost.

## What you actually get as a student subscriber

The student rate buys the same Pro account as any other subscriber:

- Full course catalog and all four career paths, including Pro-only courses such as Advanced React (18.8 hours) and Learn Responsive Web Design (15.1 hours)
- The solo projects inside the free courses, which are the Pro-marked items: Learn JavaScript's basketball scoreboard, password generator and unit converter, and Learn React's Digital Business Card and Quizzical, each with a design file and a written spec
- Unlimited interactive challenges (free accounts stop at 10 in total)
- 100 [Scrimba Explain](/docs/how-it-works/scrimba-explain/) explainers a month instead of 10 for the life of a free account
- Pro-only Discord channels (basic Discord access is free)
- Path Certificates of Completion (read the [accreditation FAQ](/docs/how-it-works/accreditation/) before you assume what these mean to employers)

## When seasonal promotions tend to appear

Beyond the student paths, Scrimba runs occasional platform-wide promotions. The pattern over recent years has been the usual SaaS calendar: a Black Friday and Cyber Monday window in late November, and an occasional New Year push in early January. These are time-boxed and the size varies year to year, so do not bank on a specific number. We track the live picture in the [2026 Scrimba discount codes guide](/blog/scrimba-discount-codes-2026/); the durable savings for students remain the GitHub Pack month and the academic-email rate, not seasonal coupons. As always, the <AffiliateLink href="https://scrimba.com/our-pricing">official pricing page</AffiliateLink> is the only authority on what is live right now.

## Honest caveats

- No public coupon code works. If you see "SCRIMBA50" or similar on a coupon site, treat it as fiction unless it is on Scrimba's own site.
- Discounts apply after verification, not at checkout. Plan for a 1 to 3 business day turnaround on the email path.
- Pricing changes. Anything specific you read here about percentage off can drift. Always confirm with support directly before you assume a number.
