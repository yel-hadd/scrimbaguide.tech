# Is Scrimba Free? Free vs Pro Explained

<DisclosureNotice />

**Yes, Scrimba is free, partially and permanently.** The free tier is not a trial: it includes {freeCount} full courses (Learn React, Learn JavaScript, Learn HTML and CSS, Learn TypeScript, Learn Python, Learn SQL, and more) plus 10 interactive challenges total. Pro unlocks all {totalCoursesLabel} courses, unlimited challenges, the 4 career paths, the Pro-only Discord channels, and path certificates. Free courses do not expire.

## Is Scrimba free? The short answer

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Is there a free plan? | Yes. A permanent free account, no card required. |
| How many courses are free? | {freeCount} of {totalCoursesLabel}, including the flagship React and JavaScript courses. |
| Is there a free trial of Pro? | No classic trial. There is a 7-day refund window and a free month for students via GitHub. See [Scrimba free trial](/docs/pricing/scrimba-free-trial/). |

| What about Scrimba Explain? | 10 explainers for life on Free, 100 a month on Pro. See the [Scrimba Explain reference](/docs/how-it-works/scrimba-explain/). |

## The free vs Pro decision (in plain English)

You do not want to pay for Pro and barely use it, or stay free and cap out at **10** challenges while you are finally gaining speed.

**What fixes it:** Match **your goal** to the lock row below. If you need a **path**, the **Pro Discord channels**, or **unlimited** challenges, Pro pays for itself in structure. If you only need one free stack (React/JS/HTML), stay Community until you feel the ceiling.

### What Pro gates inside the free courses

The gate inside a free course is the solo project: a design file, a written spec and no walkthrough. In Learn JavaScript that is the basketball scoreboard, the password generator and the unit converter, one at the end of each Practice Time module. In Learn React it is the Digital Business Card after section one and Quizzical after the final capstone. In Learn HTML and CSS three solo projects are Pro (a newsletter, an Amazon product page, a web agency hero), but the final Hometown Homepage is free.

Everything else in those courses, including every challenge and every deploy, plays on a free account.

## Choose Pro vs stay free

**Choose Pro if you:**
- Want a structured career path from zero to job-ready
- Need certificates for your resume/LinkedIn
- Want unlimited interactive challenges
- Want the Pro-only Discord channels for code reviews and instructor help
- Plan to learn consistently for 3+ months

**Stay Free if you:**
- Just want to try Scrimba's format
- Only need React or JavaScript basics
- Are supplementing another learning resource

## How far can you actually get on the free tier?

Further than most people expect. Here is a realistic free-only route, in order, before you ever hit a paywall:

1. **Learn HTML and CSS** with Per Borgen. A Google.com clone, a digital business card, a birthday site, five deploys. Budget 12 to 18 hours. No Pro needed.
2. **Learn JavaScript** with Per Borgen. A passenger counter you deploy to Netlify in module 1, then blackjack and a Chrome extension. Budget 20 to 30 hours. Free in full.
3. **Learn React** with Bob Ziroll. 15.1 hours of video, MDN-reviewed. By the end you have built Tenzies and a hangman game. Budget 35 to 45 hours.
4. **Learn TypeScript** with Bob Ziroll and Rachel Johnson. Adds types to what you already know. Budget 9 to 13 hours.
5. **Build a portfolio project** of your own using what those courses taught. This is where most of the real learning happens, and it costs nothing. The Pro solo projects (basketball scoreboard, Quizzical) do this job with a spec; on free you write your own.

That sequence is enough to assemble a small portfolio and decide whether coding is for you. **Where the free tier stops you:** you cannot follow an ordered career path end to end, you cap out at 10 interactive challenges total, you get only basic Discord access without the Pro-only channels, you earn no path certificate, and [Scrimba Explain](/docs/how-it-works/scrimba-explain/) stops after 10 lifetime explainers where Pro gets 100 a month. Those five limits are exactly what Pro removes. If you finish the route above and want a structured push to job-ready instead of a self-assembled one, that is the moment Pro starts paying for itself.

## Free or Pro? A quick decision flow

Work top to bottom and stop at the first match:

- **Just curious whether the scrim format clicks for you?** Stay Free. Open Learn React and edit live code.
- **Only need React or JavaScript fundamentals to supplement another resource?** Stay Free. The core courses cover that without a subscription.
- **Capping out at 10 challenges right when you are gaining speed?** Upgrade to Pro for unlimited challenges.
- **Want one ordered path from zero to a job-ready portfolio, plus the Pro Discord channels and a certificate?** Upgrade to annual Pro.
- **Will you study consistently for three months or more?** Annual Pro is the cheaper-per-month choice; see [Scrimba pricing](/docs/pricing/).
- **Unsure and on a tight budget?** Stay Free, ship two or three small projects, then re-decide. The decision gets obvious once you feel the ceiling.

## Scrimba free vs other free options

- **freeCodeCamp**: 100% free, text-first, certification tracks. Broader, but you code in a separate editor. See [Scrimba vs freeCodeCamp](/docs/comparisons/scrimba-vs-freecodecamp/).
- **The Odin Project**: free, reading plus projects, forces a real local environment. See [Scrimba vs The Odin Project](/docs/comparisons/scrimba-vs-odin-project/).
- **Scrimba Free**: fewer topics than either, but you code inside the lesson with no setup, which is the reason most people try it first.

## Location-based pricing

Scrimba offers **location-based discounts** that automatically adjust pricing based on your country. If you're in a lower cost-of-living region, you may see significantly reduced subscription prices, no coupon code needed. This is available on both monthly and annual plans.

Scrimba documents the policy in its help centre under [regional pricing](https://scrimba.helpscoutdocs.com/), and it is applied automatically at checkout. Check the <AffiliateLink href="https://scrimba.com/our-pricing">Scrimba pricing page</AffiliateLink> to see the pricing available in your location.

## Long-term renewal value

A single subscription keeps unlocking new material, which is why some subscribers renew across multiple years:

- **Year 1**: Complete a full career path (Frontend or Fullstack), the bulk of the value.
- **Year 2+**: Scrimba keeps adding courses (AI engineering, React 19, TypeScript), so an active subscription covers newer topics without a separate purchase.
- **After you are hired**: The catalog stays available for picking up a technology your job introduces.

For a subscriber's perspective on that ongoing value, see [Why I Keep Renewing Scrimba Pro](/blog/why-i-keep-renewing-scrimba-pro/).
