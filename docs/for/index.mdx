# Who Scrimba Is For

Scrimba fits five starting points well: complete beginners who want structure over a scattered tutorial playlist, CS students filling the gap between theory and shipped projects, designers moving into front-end code, marketers who want to build and ship without waiting on engineering, and working professionals learning on a few hours a week. Each background gets its own guide below, with where it fits and where it does not.

## Find your starting point

- **[Scrimba for absolute beginners](/docs/for/beginners/)**: Never written a line of code. A zero-intimidation first 30 days on free courses; your first build is a passenger counter you deploy to Netlify in the opening module of <AffiliateLink href="https://scrimba.com/learn-javascript-c0v">Learn JavaScript</AffiliateLink> ([our review](/docs/courses/javascript/learn-javascript/)).
- **[Scrimba for CS students](/docs/for/cs-students/)**: You have theory and algorithms but little shipped on the web. Where Scrimba closes the practical gap, and a tighter timeline. <AffiliateLink href="https://scrimba.com/learn-react-c0e">Learn React</AffiliateLink> ([our review](/docs/courses/react/learn-react/)) and <AffiliateLink href="https://scrimba.com/learn-typescript-c03c">Learn TypeScript</AffiliateLink> ([our review](/docs/courses/typescript/learn-typescript/)) are free in full.
- **[Scrimba for designers](/docs/for/designers/)**: Moving from design into front-end code. HTML, CSS, and React without losing the design sensibility you already have, plus the free 70-minute UI Design Fundamentals course.
- **[Scrimba for marketers](/docs/for/marketers/)**: Build landing pages, ship experiments, and stop waiting on engineering. Starts with the free <AffiliateLink href="https://scrimba.com/learn-to-code-with-ai-c02m">Learn to Code with AI</AffiliateLink> ([our review](/docs/courses/ai/to-code-with-ai/)) course, where you build with ChatGPT prompts.
- **[Scrimba for busy professionals](/docs/for/busy-professionals/)**: Learning on 5 to 15 hours a week around a full-time job. Most scrims are two to four minutes. Realistic schedules and how to keep momentum.

## Not sure which path follows your background?
